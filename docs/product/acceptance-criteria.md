## Verification conventions

These criteria specify future verification. SPECIFIED denotes a criterion ready for owner review. DEFERRED preserves a criterion whose scope depends on later decisions. A specified criterion can depend on controlled fixtures or an approved measurement protocol. Use synthetic targets and credentials and preserve evidence of denied effects. No acceptance test has been executed.
